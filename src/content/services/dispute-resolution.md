Callin Wild's litigation team is headed up by three highly skilled partners, Jonathan Wild, Kathryn Clough and Fletcher Craine. They have a vast range of experience in dealing with a wide range of private and commercial litigation and alternative dispute resolution.

For many years, the team has, and continues to play, a significant role in representing parties in the largest and highest value cases proceeding before the Isle of Man Courts. Callin Wild's focussed and strategic team regularly advises large international companies and individuals alike. The specialist advocates tailor their advice to each client's individual circumstances and requirements to obtain the very best result possible.

The team's work regularly involves complex cross border disputes, cross-jurisdiction asset tracing and enforcement, high value commercial disputes, insolvency matters, injunctive relief, employment law, personal injury and clinical negligence, landlord and tenant and possession claims, building and dilapidation claims and professional negligence matters.

The partners are all recognised and recommended by the leading publication, The Legal 500, for their stand-out contributions to the litigation arena on the Isle of Man.
