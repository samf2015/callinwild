The Callin Wild Private Client team provides innovative, clear and tailored solutions for Isle of Man and International based clients.

We are one of the largest dedicated Private Client teams on the Isle of Man and our advocates offer a considerate, personal service with the sensitivity that is required.

We have broad experience in dealing with all aspects of Private Client work and are proud to have the expertise of our team recognised in leading industry publications.

In addition to serving our clients on the Isle of Man, our team has extensive experience in multi-jurisdictional matters and is able to assist all clients no matter their geographical location.

Our team takes great pride in providing our clients with services and advice of the highest of standards. This level of service has led to the creation of long standing relationships with clients, advisors and industry peers all of which we are extremely proud of.

Our team is able to offer expert advice on all Private Client matters, including:

-   Wills
-   Powers of attorney
-   Grants of Probate and Letters of Administration
-   Estate Administration
-   Trusts and Settlements
-   Mental Health issues
