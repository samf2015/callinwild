Callin Wild is home to the Island's leading conveyancing team who between them hold more than 80 years' experience of Manx property transactions.

Together with their highly skilled search clerk, the conveyancing team have an unparalleled knowledge of all aspects of property sales and purchases and can also advise on any other issues involving Manx land ownership.

Partner Lesley Webb is often instructed to act in major and high-profile property deals on Island and is retained by local clients and international companies alike. Lesley regularly advises on commercial leases and commercial building sales and acquisitions.
