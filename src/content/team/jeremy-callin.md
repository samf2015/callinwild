Jeremy Callin is an Advocate, Notary Public and Commissioner for Oaths, and has been Senior Partner of Callin Wild since April 2009. Called to the Manx Bar in April 1988, he deals with a wide range of commercial and trust litigation — including mediation and arbitration — together with advisory work spanning corporate, company and insolvency law. He is an international member of the Chancery Bar Association and also maintains a substantial advisory practice in local government law and finance.

Jeremy chairs CW Corporate Services Limited, the firm's regulated corporate and trust service provider, through which he advises a range of high-net-worth individuals. He is a director of numerous client companies and trusts controlling substantial property, cash and investment portfolios, and represents clients as an advocate before the Isle of Man High Court, the Staff of Government Division and the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council.
