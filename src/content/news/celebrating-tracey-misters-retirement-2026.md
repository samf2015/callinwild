This week marks a very special moment at Callin Wild as we celebrate the retirement from full‑time employment of Tracey Mister, who has been an invaluable part of our team for almost 23 years, having started with the firm in October 2003.

Tracey has played a key role in thousands of property transactions, consistently delivering the thorough, reliable and excellent service that our clients have come to expect. Her professionalism, attention to detail, and calm expertise have made her a trusted and respected figure within the firm and across the Island’s property sector.

Last week, the partners at Callin Wild were delighted to host a drinks reception in Tracey’s honour, joined by current and former colleagues as well as members of Tracey’s family — a fitting celebration of her contribution and dedication.

Senior Partner, Jeremy Callin, shared a few words to mark the occasion - “Tracey has been an exceptional member of our team — diligent, dependable, and deeply committed to our clients. We are grateful for everything she has brought to Callin Wild, and we wish her every happiness as she takes this well‑deserved step back.”

Tracey will continue to consult for Callin Wild going forward, but will be taking that deserved step back from Friday, 26 June. While we will miss her greatly in the office, we are delighted for her as she begins this next chapter.
