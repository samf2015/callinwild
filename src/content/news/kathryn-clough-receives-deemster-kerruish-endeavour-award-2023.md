In September 2023 Kathryn Clough, a Partner at Callin Wild was awarded the Deemster Kerruish Endeavour Award owing to her huge dedication to the Isle of Man Law Society during her time on the Council, Vice-Presidency, and Presidency from 2015-2023.

During her time on the Law Society Council, and particularly as Vice- President and President, Kathryn navigated the profession through the COVID pandemic and the Legal Services Review for the benefit of the membership and the public. Kathryn was also pivotal in updating the Law Society’s Constitution to ensure the continued relevance of the Society and ensured that the staff and members of Council were fully supported at all times.

The Endeavour Award is made in memory of His Honour First Deemster Michael Kerruish and was presented to Kathryn by Marianne Kerruish and His Honour Geoffrey Tattersall KC.
