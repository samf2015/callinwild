Callin Wild are thrilled to sponsor the Isle of Man Women’s Golf Team for the 2025 Island Games, where twenty-four island groups from across the world are set gather in Orkney to compete across twelve sports next week. We are proud to support local sport on an international stage and supply the team with kit for the 72-hole competition.

One of the members of the team is our very own Breeshey Jansen! Breeshey is a trainee in the Litigation Department at Callin Wild and over recent months balancing work with practicing her swing!

All of us at Callin Wild wish the team and our trainee advocate, Breeshey, every success at the upcoming Island Games!
