At Callin Wild, we believe in supporting our local community, particularly through sport — and that includes investing in the next generation. We’re proud to announce our recent sponsorship of a brand-new football kit for the students of Marown School.

The new kit was officially handed over by Fletcher Craine, Partner at Callin Wild, during a visit to the school last week. The children were excited to receive their new kit and wore it in a competition the same afternoon!

“Sport plays a vital role in the development of young people, teaching teamwork and confidence,” said Fletcher. “We’re delighted to help the school with the new kit and everyone at Callin Wild wishes the Marown School football team the very best of luck for their upcoming matches!”
