We are delighted to announce that Julia Crellin has been called to the Manx Bar after successfully completed her articles.

Julia joined Callin Wild in 2022, bringing with her a wealth of experience in trust and company administration within the fiduciary services and Private Client sectors.

Julia’s achievement marks a significant milestone in her legal career and her success in passing the Manx Bar examinations, whilst caring for her young family, is a testament to her hard work and dedication.

Marc Furness, Partner at Callin Wild and Julia’s Principal, expressed his pride in Julia’s accomplishment, stating:

"I am delighted to congratulate Julia on being called to the Manx Bar. We are proud to have her as part of the Callin Wild team and look forward to seeing her continue to excel in her legal career"

(Julia is pictured with His Honour Deemster Corlett and Marc Furness, and also with all the Partners at Callin Wild)
