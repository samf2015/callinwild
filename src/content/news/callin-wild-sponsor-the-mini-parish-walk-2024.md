Callin Wild were once again delighted to sponsor this years ‘Mini Parish Walk’ with over £12,000 being raised for 37 different charities.

The walk, which was hosted by the Douglas Rotary Club at St John’s arboretum on Saturday, saw just under 200 children aged from 4 to 11 walking distances ranging from one to two and a half kilometres.

The route is set around the ‘Parish’ signs in the Arboretum and each child was awarded with a medal and certificate. Thank you to all the volunteer marshals that helped guide the children round the course and well done to all who took part!
