Callin Wild is proud to announce its sponsorship of the Douglas Royal U12s football team for the 2025-2026 season. This partnership reflects the firm’s ongoing commitment to supporting local community initiatives and fostering youth development through sports.

The Douglas Royal U12s will proudly wear kits bearing the Callin Wild logo during their matches in the Isle of Man Junior Football League.

“We are delighted to support the Douglas Royal U12s,” said Marc Furness, a partner at Callin Wild. “At Callin Wild, we believe in nurturing the next generation, and this sponsorship is a fantastic opportunity to encourage teamwork, discipline, and a passion for sport among these young people.”
