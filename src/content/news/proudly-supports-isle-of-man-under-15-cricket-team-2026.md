Callin Wild is proud to sponsor the Isle of Man Cricket Under-15 team as they continue to develop the next generation of Manx cricket talent. The company's support has helped the young squad represent the Isle of Man on the international stage, most recently at the ICC Europe Under-15 Festival held at King's College, Taunton.

The festival brought together some of Europe's most promising young cricketers, providing an outstanding opportunity for the Isle of Man players to test themselves against strong international opposition while gaining invaluable experience both on and off the field. Competing in a high-quality environment allowed the squad to showcase their skills, build confidence, and create lasting memories.

Callin Wild believes that investing in youth sport helps develop not only talented athletes but also teamwork, resilience, discipline, and leadership which are all qualities that will benefit these young players throughout their lives.

The Isle of Man Cricket Association continues to provide outstanding opportunities for young cricketers to progress through the pathway to representative and international cricket, with events such as the ICC Europe Under-15 Festival playing an important role in that development.

Everyone at Callin Wild congratulates the players, coaches, volunteers, and families who made the trip to Taunton possible. We are delighted to support the team's journey and look forward to following their continued progress as they represent the Isle of Man with pride.
