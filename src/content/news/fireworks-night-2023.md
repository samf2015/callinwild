2nd Onchan Scout Group recently held their annual Group Firework Night on the 1st November 2023. Over 100 young people and their families attended the display in Onchan which has been running for many years.

Different ages from across the group took part in activities prior to the display with Beavers learning about Fireworks Night to the Scouts hiking from Groudle to the Headquarters of 2nd Onchan where the display took place.

The display was a chance for the whole group to meet together to celebrate Fireworks Night and after the display had finished, the obligatory hotdogs and soup were enjoyed by all.

Marc Furness, Partner commented ‘Callin Wild is committed to the local community on the Isle of Man and the 2nd Onchan Group Firework Night demonstrates an excellent community project giving back to their members and families in a fun way and I am pleased that we are able to support such an event’.

Scouting is shaped by young people in partnership with adults and scouts on the Isle of Man are always looking for new volunteers and if you fancy a go, why not join the adventure at [www.scouts.im/join](http://www.scouts.im/join)
